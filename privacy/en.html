TwoTap Reminders - Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

TwoTap Reminders ("the app") is made by NeatBit. This page explains which data the app uses and where it goes. In short: your reminders never leave your phone. The only data that goes online is what Google needs to show ads in the free version and to handle the Pro purchase.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Reminders, notes and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. The app has no account and no servers of its own: NeatBit never receives them.
  • Backups (exported by you, or automatic in the Pro version) are files saved in the Download folder of your phone. They go somewhere else only if you move or share them yourself.
  • "Pin notifications" (optional, Pro): if you give the app Android's notification access, it reads the notifications on your phone (title, text and the app they come from) only when you tap "Pin notifications", to turn them into reminders. The content of your notifications stays on your phone: it is never sent anywhere, shared or used for ads.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a small banner at the bottom of some screens, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads library collects and sends to Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • the IP address, which may be used to estimate the approximate location of the device;
  • interactions with the app and with ads (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic data (for example app launch time and performance).

The data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form the first time it needs to show an ad; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). With the Pro version the app shows no ads and does not request them.

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

The Pro purchase

The Pro version is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled only by Google: the app only learns whether the Pro purchase is active on your Google account, so it can unlock the Pro features. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Exact alarms: so that reminders go off at the right minute.
  • Run at startup: to set the alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: only for ads and the Pro purchase.
  • Notification access (optional, only if you turn it on): for "Pin notifications", as described above.
  • Advertising ID and Google Play billing: added by the Google libraries for ads and for the purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved on your phone, except the backup files in the Download folder, which you can delete yourself. Since NeatBit holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side; for the data collected by Google for ads, see the Google links above.
